Reporting Persons can now view their assigned team members directly within the system, improving team coordination and visibility.  

The Team Member View feature enables Reporting Persons (RP) to view their assigned team members directly within the system. This enhances visibility, improves team coordination, and makes it easier to manage team-related information. 

**How to Access**

- Log in to the HRMS Dashboard  

- Navigate to the Employment tab  

- Click on the My Team tab  

- View the list of assigned team members  

Use the Search option to find specific team members if the list is large.
