The **Appraisal Reports** module allows HR to generate and export employee appraisal data, including performance scores, ratings, and comments.

**Accessing the Page**

1. Click on the **Reports** tab.  
2. Select **Appraisal Reports** from the menu.  
3. The **Report Generator** screen will be displayed.


**Generating an Appraisal Report**

- On the **Report Generator** page, click on the **Appraisal** card.  
- The **Appraisal Report Filter** popup will appear.

- Use the following filters to refine the report:

    - Department
    - Year
    - Employee Selection

- After applying the required filters, click **Generate Report**.  
- The system will process the request and generate the report **Excel format (.xlsx)**.
