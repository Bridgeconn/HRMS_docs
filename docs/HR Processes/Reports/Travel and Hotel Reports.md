The **Travel & Hotel Reports** module allows HR users to generate and export travel and hotel booking data for employees.

**Accessing the Page**

1. Click on the **Reports** tab.  
2. Select **Travel & Hotel Reports** from the menu.  
3. The **Report Generator** screen will be displayed.

**Travel Report**

Provides a complete history of employee travel requests.

**Includes:**
- Travel request details  
- Pending and approved requests  
- Employee travel history  

**Steps to Generate:**
1. Click on the **Travel** card.  
2. Apply the required filters.  
3. Click **Generate Report**.

**Hotel Report**

Provides a complete history of hotel booking requests.

**Steps to Generate:**
1. Click on the **Hotel** card.  
2. Apply the required filters.  
3. Click **Generate Report**.

- The report is generated in **Excel format (.xlsx)**  
- Sent to the **registered email address** of the logged-in HR user
