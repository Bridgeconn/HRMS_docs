The Report Generator page allows HR to export employee-related data in Excel format and receive it via email. It provides two main report options: Employee Profile and Employee List.

**Accessing the Page**

Click on the Reports tab.
Select Employee Reports from the menu.
The Report Generator screen will be displayed with the available report options.

**Available Reports**

**1. Employee Profile**

This report allows HR to export detailed information for employees.

**Features**

- Choose specific data categories:
- Personal Details
- Contact Information
- Bank Details
- All details combined
- Data is compiled into a single Excel file.

**How to Generate**

- Select the required data category.
- Click “Exports & Sends to Email”.
- The report will be generated and sent to your registered email.

**2.Employee List**

This report provides a summary list of employees.

Features:

**How to Generate**

- Choose the employee type (Active or Resigned).
- Click “Exports & Sends to Email”.
- The report will be generated and sent to your registered email.
